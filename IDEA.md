# Dream

Dream is a design application that is intuitive, simple and elegant. It has lots of features but it does not overwhelm its users by throwing everything on their face. It is inspired by the simplicity of Microsoft Paint, which comes bundled within Windows, which is so simple to use that even the users with limited comfort using technology can build or manage many things. It is deep enough to provide many image editing features that come in Photoshop. It is intelligent enough to create and modify both whole images and selective parts of the images using AI. It is cognizant enough that it can provide feedback to the user along with suggestions and actionable steps. Users can selectively enter design mode, where layers and components are made available, and what they draw can be made reusable across their other designs. It is powerful enough that the users can convert their drawings, images, designs to animations, presentations, videos and games by providing conversing with their work. It is ambitious enough to let users create whole applications with just their drawings, images, designs.

The heart of the app is simplicity and usability. Even a 5 year old should be able to use it productively. Even a 90 year old should be able to express themselves effectively. Literacy is also not a constraint, the app is visual and graphical enough to guide anyone. Intuition as we should say. Dream feels natural. Like how there are no constraints & bounds in dreams, Dream brings that space to the users, a canvas that is so natural that it doesn’t need teaching.

The personas therefore are anyone. But to give an example

2. Victor, 85 years old, Irish main, who is retired now for 20 years from his civil engineering job, and wants to bring out his imagination on canvas
3. Ali, 30 years old, Pakistani man, who is a professional artist, uses digital pen and other equipment to produce digital arts, has a philosophy to blend real art and digital arts
4. Fatima, 21 years old, Iranian woman, who is studying medicine, has a passion for Islamic calligraphy, who wants to experiment with different ideas to bring innovation to calligraphy
5. George, 45 years old, American man, who is a farmer and did not get any education, lives in countryside, has bought a new phone, and wants to create dream like animations with what they are feeling
6. Sara, 15 years old, Nigerian teenage girl, goes to high school, has a part time freelance work to design logos as a side earning
7. Zǐxuān, 28 years old, Chinese man, who is pursuing his PhD in Chemistry, wants to create scientific images, animations and apps
8. Maria, 32 years old, Brazilian woman, who is a professional programmer, and has advanced in Agentic AI based coding, and wants to bring professional graphics design work and a taste to the applications she develops, wants to also connect the tools she uses using MCP in development flow and APIs in applications

Dream is free of cost for all the features, with AI based features having limited free interactions and much greater usage for paid plans. Users can bring in their own AI stack and connect, like Claude Code, Codex, or various models, which can enhance the functionality and in many cases waive the pricing plan.
